3 Ingredient Ice Cream (No Machine, No Churn)

3 ingredient no-churn vanilla ice cream: no ice cream machine, no churning, just whipped cream folded into sweetened condensed milk. INGREDIENTS (12 servings, about 6 cups / 1.4 L) - 1 can (14 oz / 397 g) sweetened condensed milk - 2 cups (480 ml) cold heavy cream - 2 tsp (10 ml) pure vanilla extract - a pinch of fine salt (free extra)

Steps

  1. Chill a 9x5x3 in (23x13x8 cm) metal loaf pan in the freezer.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk the condensed milk, vanilla and salt.
  3. Whip the cold cream with a mixer on medium-high until firm peaks form, about 2 minutes.
  4. Fold about 1 cup of the whipped cream into the condensed milk mixture, then fold that back into the rest of the cream until well blended.
  5. Spread it in the chilled pan, cover and freeze about 2 hours, until thick like soft serve.
  6. Freeze, covered, about 3 hours more, until solid and scoopable.

Active 15 minutes, total about 5 hours 15 minutes. Serves 12.

Allergens: contains milk.
