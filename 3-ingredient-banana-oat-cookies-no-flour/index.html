3 Ingredient Banana Oat Cookies (No Flour)

3 ingredient banana oat cookies: a rescue for overripe bananas, with no flour, no butter and no eggs. INGREDIENTS (8 cookies) - 2 ripe bananas (the browner, the sweeter) - 1 cup (100 g) rolled oats - 1/4 cup (45 g) chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350 F (180 C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, mash the bananas until smooth, then mix in the oats until well combined.
  3. Stir in the chocolate chips.
  4. Scoop 8 cookies onto the sheet and flatten each one a little (they do not spread).
  5. Bake 12 minutes. Let them cool a few minutes on the sheet.

Prep 10 minutes, bake 12 minutes, total 22 minutes. Makes 8 cookies.

Allergens: oats (use certified gluten-free oats if needed); chocolate chips may contain milk and soy.
