3 Ingredient Mac and Cheese (One Pot, No Draining)

3 ingredient mac and cheese in one pot: the pasta cooks right in the milk, so there is nothing to drain. An easy side for Thanksgiving or a quick dinner. INGREDIENTS (4 servings) - 5 cups (1.25 L) milk - 1 lb (455 g) dry elbow macaroni - 2 cups (200 g) shredded cheddar cheese

Steps

  1. In a large pot, bring the milk to a boil.
  2. Add the macaroni and stir constantly until the pasta is cooked, about 10 minutes, so the milk does not stick or boil over.
  3. Turn off the heat, add the cheddar and stir until the cheese is melted and the pasta is evenly coated.
  4. Serve right away.

Why it works: the starch from the pasta stays in the milk and thickens it into a sauce, so you need no flour and no butter.

Prep 5 minutes, cook 10 minutes, total 15 minutes. Serves 4.

Allergens: contains milk and wheat (gluten).
