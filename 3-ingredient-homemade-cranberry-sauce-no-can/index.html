3 Ingredient Homemade Cranberry Sauce (No Can)

3 ingredient homemade cranberry sauce for Thanksgiving: the classic recipe from the cranberry bag, and the one step that makes it set. INGREDIENTS (about 2 1/4 cups) - 1 bag (12 oz / 340 g) fresh or frozen cranberries, rinsed - 1 cup (200 g) sugar - 1 cup (240 ml) water

Steps

  1. In a medium saucepan, bring the sugar and water to a boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Add the cranberries and bring it back to a boil.
  3. Turn the heat down and simmer gently for 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until the berries pop.
  4. Take it off the heat, cover and let it cool completely at room temperature. It looks thin while hot and thickens as it cools.
  5. Refrigerate until serving.

Prep 5 minutes, cook 15 minutes, plus cooling. Makes about 2 1/4 cups. Make it a day or two ahead and keep it covered in the fridge.

Careful: hot sugar syrup spatters, keep the simmer gentle.

Why it works: cranberries hold natural pectin. When the berries pop, the pectin goes into the syrup and sets the sauce as it cools.
