2 Ingredient Dough Bagels (No Yeast, No Boiling)

2 ingredient dough bagels: self-rising flour and Greek yogurt make the dough, with no yeast, no boiling and no rising time. An egg wash and everything seasoning finish them (4 ingredients in all). INGREDIENTS (4 bagels) - 1 3/4 cups (220 g) self-rising flour, plus a little more for dusting - 1 cup (245 g) Greek yogurt - 1 egg, beaten - 4 tbsp everything seasoning, to taste

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400 F (200 C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the flour and yogurt until they come together into a ball.
  3. On a lightly floured surface, flatten the dough into a disc about 8 in (20 cm) wide and cut it into 4 equal pieces.
  4. Roll each piece into a rope about 8 in (20 cm) long, shape it into a ring and pinch the ends together. Move the rings to the sheet.
  5. Brush the tops with the beaten egg and sprinkle on the seasoning.
  6. Bake 20 to 25 minutes, until golden brown. Let them cool a few minutes.

Prep 15 minutes, bake 25 minutes, total 40 minutes. Makes 4 bagels.

Allergens: wheat (gluten), milk, egg; everything seasoning usually contains sesame.
