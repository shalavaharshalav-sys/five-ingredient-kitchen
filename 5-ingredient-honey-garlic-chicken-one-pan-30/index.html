5 Ingredient Honey Garlic Chicken (One Pan, 30 Minutes)

5 ingredient honey garlic chicken: crispy chicken thighs in a sticky honey, garlic and soy glaze, all in one pan in about 30 minutes. INGREDIENTS (4 servings) - 8 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (about 2.5 lb / 1.1 kg) - 6 garlic cloves, thinly sliced - 3 tbsp (45 ml) honey - 3 tbsp (45 ml) white wine vinegar - 3 tbsp (45 ml) reduced-salt soy sauce - free extras: 1 tbsp (15 ml) olive oil, salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a large saute pan over medium-high heat. Season the chicken with salt and pepper.
  2. Fry the thighs skin side down 6 to 8 minutes, until the skin is deep golden.
  3. Flip them. Add the garlic, honey, vinegar and soy sauce and let it bubble.
  4. Simmer 11 to 13 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until the thickest thigh reads 165 F (74 C) on an instant-read thermometer.
  5. Serve with the sticky sauce from the pan (rice is great with it).

Total about 30 minutes. Serves 4.

Allergens: contains soy and wheat (soy sauce; use tamari for gluten free).
