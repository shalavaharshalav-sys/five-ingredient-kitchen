3 Ingredient Peanut Butter Cookies (No Flour)

3 ingredient peanut butter cookies with no flour: one bowl, 20 minutes, about 25 cookies.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (240 g) peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup (100 g) sugar
  • 1 large egg

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (180°C).
  2. In a large bowl, mix the peanut butter, sugar and egg until smooth.
  3. Roll a spoonful of dough into a ball and place it on a nonstick or parchment-lined baking sheet.
  4. Flatten each ball with a fork, then press again the other way for a criss-cross pattern.
  5. Bake 8 to 10 minutes, until the bottoms are golden brown.
  6. Let the cookies cool; they firm up as they cool.

Makes about 25 cookies. Prep 10 minutes, bake 10 minutes.

Allergens: contains peanuts and egg.
